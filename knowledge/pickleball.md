# Pickleball: what good form looks like

This file is sent with every review. The Review agent judges the clip against it, and every
tip names one cue from the list below. A coach must check this file before real players use
the product (PRD open question 2).

## Cues (use these exact words in the `cue` field)

| Cue | Good form | Common mistake |
| --- | --- | --- |
| quiet wrist | The wrist stays firm. The shoulder and legs move the paddle. | The wrist flicks at contact, so depth and height change shot to shot. |
| flat face | The paddle face is flat or a little open at contact and stays that way through the ball. | The face opens wide at contact, so the ball floats up (a pop-up). |
| soft knees | Knees stay bent and the player stays low through the shot. | The legs straighten while reaching, so the player lifts the ball. |
| out in front | Contact is in front of the body, ahead of the front hip. | Contact is beside or behind the hip, so the player is late and pushes up. |
| short swing | The swing is compact, about the size of a shoebox, with a short follow-through. | A big backswing or a fast upward follow-through. |
| paddle up | Between shots the paddle is up, in front of the chest. | The paddle drops to the knees between shots. |
| split step | A small hop to land balanced as the opponent hits. | Flat feet, or still moving when the opponent hits. |
| walk it in | After a drop, the player moves forward toward the kitchen line. | The player stays at the baseline after a good drop. |
| arc and land soft | The ball travels on a soft arc and lands in the kitchen. | A flat, hard ball that sails deep or into the net. |
| small steps | Short shuffle steps, feet do not cross. | Reaching with the arm instead of moving the feet. |
| loose grip | Grip pressure about 3 out of 10 for soft shots. | A tight grip, so the ball comes off the paddle hot. |
| soak it up | On hard balls, the paddle gives a little to absorb pace. | Punching at hard balls, so they come back high. |

## Moves

- **Dink:** a soft shot from the kitchen line that lands in the opponent's kitchen. Key cues: quiet wrist, flat face, soft knees, out in front, short swing.
- **Third-shot drop:** a soft shot from the baseline that lands in the kitchen so the player can move up. Key cues: arc and land soft, walk it in, quiet wrist.
- **Serve:** underhand, contact below the waist. Key cues: short swing, loose grip.
- **Return:** deep return, then move to the kitchen line. Key cues: walk it in, split step.
- **Volley:** ball hit before it bounces, at the kitchen line. Key cues: paddle up, soak it up, short swing.
- **Drive:** a hard, low shot. Key cues: out in front, short swing, flat face.

## Level notes

- **Casual:** focus on one or two big things. Keep words simple.
- **Regular:** consistency, footwork and the soft game.
- **Competitive:** small details, shot choice under pressure, and resets.

## Rules for the coach

- Look at the player, not at other people on court. Say who you judged.
- Only give a tip for something you can see at that moment in the clip. If you are not sure, give no tip for it.
- Never give medical or injury advice.
